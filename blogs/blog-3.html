Modern Marketing: Why Are Brands Paying to Get Roasted?

By Fardin Rahman Khan Raafi | Marketing Strategy & Brand Psychology

Think about it. We don't have the patience to watch a 5-second ad on YouTube. We literally sit with our fingers hovering over the mouse, eagerly waiting for the "Skip" button to appear.

Yet, those same people will happily sit down to watch a 2-hour roast show where the sponsor brands are ruthlessly made fun of! And the crazy part? Brands are paying top dollar just to be insulted.

This is what I call Modern Marketing. It sounds absurd, but the game of product marketing has completely shifted.

The Power Shift: Who Holds the Control Now?

Once upon a time, brands had full control over the narrative. They produced highly polished videos, wrote perfect scripts, and bought our attention with feature-heavy product pitches. But the tables have turned. Today, the power belongs entirely to the audience.

Gen Z and modern internet audiences absolutely despise the traditional corporate monologue. If your content feels even slightly "promotional" or fake, they will scroll past it in a microsecond. They don't come online to listen to brand lectures; they come to enjoy the moment.

Case Study: India’s Got Latent

Samay Raina & Internet Culture

Take Samay Raina’s India’s Got Latent as a prime example. Premiering across YouTube and Netflix, it has taken the internet by storm.

Why is this show so incredibly popular? Because it is raw, unpredictable, and perfectly aligned with meme culture. Startup founders come onto the show specifically to get roasted. Brands become part of the inside jokes. People don't view these integrations as advertisements; they share them organically as pure entertainment.

Influencer Marketing vs. Culture-Led Marketing

Many marketers might brush this off as just another influencer marketing strategy. It is not. This is culture-led marketing! When you successfully integrate your product into an active trend or culture, people start viewing your brand as an extension of their own identity.

You can no longer earn attention just by shouting "We are the best." Attention is earned when you naturally become part of an interesting conversation.

The Ultimate Framework for Modern Attention

If your brand wants to survive and thrive in this era, you have to break some old rules:

1. Stop the Corporate Monologue

Consumers do not care about your list of product features. They care about relevance.

2. Be Interesting Enough to Share

Do something bold that makes people want to create memes about you and discuss your brand in their private group chats.

3. Embrace the Raw Format

An authentic, unpolished, and raw approach is far more effective today than a perfectly manicured corporate aesthetic.

Truth be told, the very definition of marketing has evolved. Marketing no longer means interrupting people's entertainment to show an ad.

Marketing itself must become the entertainment.
