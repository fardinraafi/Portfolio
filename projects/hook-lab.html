How the score works

No AI, no API, nothing leaves your browser. Just the copywriting rules I use every day, turned into a formula.

🧩

Curiosity · 22%

Open loops, questions, twists and cliffhanger endings that make people click "see more".

✂️

Clarity & length · 20%

Fits before the platform cuts it off, with no buzzwords, hedging or long words.

🎯

Specificity · 17%

Numbers, money, timeframes and names. Specific beats vague every time.

🔥

Emotion · 15%

Power words, stakes and honest stories. One or two emojis, not ten.

⚡

Pattern interrupt · 14%

Avoids "excited to announce" energy. Short, bold first sentences win.

👀

Reader focus · 12%

Talks to "you", opens with a command, and makes the reader the main character.
