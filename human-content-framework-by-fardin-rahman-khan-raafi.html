The Human-Centric
AI Content Framework

How I write content that sounds human. My exact step-by-step process for leveraging AI tools to bypass the "bot tone" and create genuine connections.

01

The Raw Draft (Zero AI)

First, make a draft of what you want to write. It doesn't need to be fact-checked or perfectly structured. Just write. Do not use any AI in this stage. The first draft MUST come completely from your brain.

02

The AI Argument

Feed the draft into an AI and give it context. Tell it why you are writing this, your end goal, and instruct the AI to argue with you about the idea to find loopholes and test your logic.

03

The Reconstruction Prompt

Now, take your raw draft and whatever the AI argued about, and paste this exact prompt to rebuild the content:

Use my first draft, combine it with your arguments, and remake me a new content without changing the tone, approach, style, language, or humor from my original draft.
04

The Polish (or Anti-Polish)

Sometimes perfection is the enemy of authentic. Make intentional mistakes. When you do need to clean up inconsistencies, use this prompt:

From the content clean any grammatical mistakes, errors, and inconsistency. Do not use Em dashes, do not repeat words... [add specific rules here].

Or, use dedicated grammar tools:

Grammarly QuillBot
05

Read. Every. Single. Word.

This is crucial. You need to read the whole content word by word before posting anywhere. Only a human can understand if content feels natural. Stop trying to be the "best writer." Use emotion, cite your sources, and make it sound like YOU.

💡 Quick Humanizer Tips

  • Make it personal: Nobody's ever gotten attached to "leveraging synergies."
  • Use your trademark move: Include the thing only you would say.
  • Be funny: If it wouldn't survive being read out loud at brunch, cut it.
  • Drop a niche reference: Don't explain it. If they get it, they get it.
