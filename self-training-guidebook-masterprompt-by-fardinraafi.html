Master Prompt: Personalized Skill Guidebook Generator
No blanks to fill in yourself — just paste this prompt into Claude as-is. Claude will ask you a few quick questions, then build your full personal learning guidebook. 

Act as a patient, practical mentor building me a personal skill-learning guidebook.

Before you write anything, ask me the following questions one at a time (or in a short batch if that's easier for me to answer), and wait for my answers before building the guidebook. Keep the questions simple and give examples so I know how to answer:

1. What's your name?
2. What's your current field of study or background? (e.g. Finance major, CS student, Marketing student)
3. What's your career goal — what kind of desk job or role do you want in the future?
4. What skills or tools do you already want to learn? (e.g. MS Excel/Google Sheets, MS Access, Power BI, SQL, Python, etc. — list as many as you want, or just say "not sure, suggest for me" and I'll let you recommend the right mix based on my career goal)
5. What's your current skill level in your top-priority tool? (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced — if I'm not sure, offer to ask me 2-3 quick questions to assess it)
6. How much time can I realistically practice per day?
7. How do I learn best? (e.g. simple plain English, step-by-step with screenshots, video-first, learn-by-doing)

Once I've answered, build a complete guidebook that includes:

- A daily practice routine for my #1 priority tool (something I repeat every day, not a one-time lesson).
- For each practice session, give me the exact prompt I should copy-paste into Claude to generate a fresh practice worksheet + explanation, covering one function/skill at a time. Use this structure for the prompts you give me:
  "Make me a practice exercise on [FUNCTION/TOPIC] in [TOOL]. Include 5-8 sample problems with realistic data, the correct formulas/steps, and a plain-English explanation of when and why I'd use this in a real job."
- Cover the essential functions/skills in a logical learning order (e.g. for Excel: SUM, AVERAGE, COUNTIF, IF, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, INDEX-MATCH, Pivot Tables, Power Query, etc. — adjust for whichever tools I listed).
- A structured multi-week roadmap — break my chosen skills into phases (Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced), with what to focus on each week.
- YouTube channel recommendations with names and hyperlinks for each tool I listed, prioritizing channels known for clear, practical, job-relevant tutorials.
- Other useful resources: free courses, cheat sheets, practice datasets, communities/forums, and any certifications worth having for my career goal.
- A glossary of abbreviations and terms I'll run into (e.g. VBA, ETL, KPI, DAX, PQ, ODBC — whatever's relevant to my chosen tools), explained in simple language.
- Bonus suggestions — if there are other tools, software, or skills (beyond what I listed) that would meaningfully help someone with my background reach my career goal, suggest them and briefly explain why.

Keep the tone simple, encouraging, and beginner-friendly — no jargon without explanation.
