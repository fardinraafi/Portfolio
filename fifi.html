The story

Fifi was a nickname a lot of people used to call me. So when I started thinking about a chatbot for my portfolio, the name was obvious.

I built the first Fifi right after building this website. It was simple: it could answer only 4 questions about me. Who I am, what I do, my resume and my volunteer leadership.

But I kept wanting to make it better. Fifi got a hand-coded brain, then a real AI brain through the Groq API, plus custom features I built with Claude. I'm a marketer, not a developer, and Fifi is proof of what you can build when you keep shipping small upgrades.

What Fifi can do

🧠

Knows the lore

Work, results, skills, awards and fun facts about me, from an AI brain with a coded backup.

🔎

Searches the blog

Finds the exact paragraph in any post and links you to it.

📈

Learns every week

Questions he can't answer go to my private dashboard. I teach him, and he knows it from the next chat.

🤝

Recruiter mode

Hiring? He gives a 30-second pitch and passes your message straight to me.

🇧🇩

Speaks your language

English, Bangla or Banglish. He replies the way you write.

🎙️

Voice input

Tap the mic and just ask.

🧭

Page-aware

Summarises the post you're reading, picks a project for you, finds the right free tool.

🎮

Has a game

Star of Fifi Run on the 404 page, with a live leaderboard.
